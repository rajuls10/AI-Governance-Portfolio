This repository documents my learning journey from Python basics to advanced AI — structured around real-world AI governance problems.

Every technical concept is connected to a governance question. The goal is building the technical literacy to understand, analyze, and govern AI systems.

---

## What This Portfolio Is

Three real-world case studies run throughout the entire learning journey:

- 🔴 **NCII (Primary)** — Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery generated by AI image platforms. How do generative AI systems enable gender-based harm, and what does meaningful governance look like technically and legally?
- 🟡 **Biometric Surveillance** — Facial recognition in law enforcement. Where does bias enter the pipeline, and what do high-risk AI obligations under the EU AI Act require?
- 🟢 **Recruitment AI** — Automated hiring systems that discriminate. How does algorithmic bias manifest mathematically, and what governance frameworks exist to address it?

---

## Frameworks Used Throughout

- **EU AI Act** — Risk tiers, high-risk obligations, prohibited practices, transparency requirements
- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework** — Govern, Map, Measure, Manage
- **UNESCO Recommendation on AI Ethics** — Human rights framing, gender equality
- **CEDAW** — Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination Against Women
